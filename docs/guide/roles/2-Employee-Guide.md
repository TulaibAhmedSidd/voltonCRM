# Volton CRM — Employee Guide

For: **Call agents**, **Field agents** and **staff with a custom role** (e.g. Installer, Store). Website: https://volton-crm.vercel.app

> **The golden rule:** every call and WhatsApp must start **from the CRM buttons**, and you must **save what happened** with a screenshot. That is your proof of work.

---

## 1. First day — checklist (everyone)

- [ ] Sign in with the username and temporary password from your manager, then set your own password.
- [ ] Install the app: Android Chrome → **Install app**; iPhone Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- [ ] Tap the bell 🔔 → **Turn on phone / PC notifications**. New leads, pings and reminders come to your phone even when the CRM is closed.
- [ ] Find your section below: call agent (2), field agent (3), or staff / custom role (4).

---

## 2. Call agents

### 2.1 Start of the day
- [ ] Open **Dashboard** → **Check in**. You get **no leads** until you check in.
- [ ] Going for lunch / prayer: **Start break** (new leads pause). Back: **End break**.
- [ ] End of day: **Check out** → **Yes, check out**. If you forget, the CRM checks you out 30 minutes after office closes.

### 2.2 When a new lead comes
1. Your phone shows **"New lead for you — accept now"**.
2. Dashboard → **Accept VL-xxxxx** (or open the lead → **Accept this lead**).
3. Accept within **5 minutes**. If you don't, your manager is told and the lead may go to the next agent.
4. The customer's number is hidden until you accept.

### 2.3 Contacting the customer (one "try")
1. On the lead page tap **WhatsApp**, **WA call** or **Call**. The CRM records the time and opens WhatsApp / the dialer.
2. Talk to the customer.
3. Come back to the CRM. A box asks **"What happened?"**:
   - **Step 1 — Did you reach the customer?** Connected · No Answer · Busy · Number Off · Wrong Number · Couldn't call now
   - **Step 2 (if connected) — What did the customer say?** Interested · Not Interested · Call Back Requested · Already has solar · Deal done (enter sale value)
   - Note, next follow-up time (optional)
   - **Add a screenshot** of the call log or chat — best proof
4. Press **Save result**.

Tapped by mistake? Use **"I tapped by mistake — no call or chat happened"** (within 2 minutes). It is not counted as a try.
You cannot start a new try on a lead until the last one is saved.

### 2.4 The 3-try rule
- Try 1 → Try 2 (next day) → Try 3 (3 days later). The CRM reminds you: **"Follow-up due"**.
- **3 no-answers** (No Answer / Busy / Number Off) over at least 2 days → the lead becomes **Dead** automatically.
- **Wrong Number** → Junk.
- Missing a follow-up by 4 hours → **OVERDUE** alert goes to you **and your manager**.

### 2.5 Moving the lead forward
| You do | Lead moves to |
|---|---|
| Connected call | Contacted |
| Customer said Interested | Interested |
| Save **Site & visit → Site details** (bill, units, roof, kW) | Requirement Collected |
| **Book site visit** | Site Survey (a field agent is chosen automatically) |
| **Quotation → Generate quotation PDF** with "Move to Quotation sent" ticked | Quotation Sent |

You can also set Contacted / Interested / Requirement Collected yourself in **Stage**.

### 2.6 Making a quotation
1. Lead page → **Quotation** card.
2. Choose **PDF design**: **Volton classic** (our usual PDF) or **Modern**.
3. Fill panels, inverter, battery, structure, wiring, net metering, labour, extras, discount, warranty. The total updates live.
4. **Generate quotation PDF** → then **Send to customer on WhatsApp**, **Download**, or **Share from phone**.
   (Sending from the CRM works only if the customer messaged in the last 24 hours — otherwise use **Open customer's WhatsApp**.)

### 2.7 Closing a lead
- **Deal done** → enter the sale value. **Not interested / Dead / Wrong number** → write what the customer said (required).
- Tick **Close this lead (your manager will check it)**.
- Your manager checks every close. If they **Dispute**, the lead re-opens and you are told.

### 2.8 Things that get flagged (avoid them)
Never left the app · Too fast · Screenshot reused · Screenshot time mismatch · Logged while not checked in · Call length much longer than the time away. Some clean calls are randomly **spot-checked** — your manager may call the customer.

### 2.9 Daily checklist — call agent
- [ ] Checked in?
- [ ] All new leads accepted within 5 minutes?
- [ ] Every new lead contacted within 15 minutes?
- [ ] Every try saved with a result and a screenshot?
- [ ] **Follow-ups** page: nothing overdue?
- [ ] Interested customers: site details saved / visit booked / quotation sent?
- [ ] Checked out at the end of the day?

## 3. Field agents (site visits)

- [ ] **Check in** when you start field work — checked-in field agents get new visits first.
- [ ] Your phone shows **"Site visit: <customer> (<kW>)"**. Open **Site visits**.
- [ ] Go to the site, then press **Update visit** and choose: **Visited · Interested · Not interested · Rescheduled**.
- [ ] Write **What happened?** (required). For Rescheduled, set the new time.
- [ ] **Interested** moves the lead to Quotation Pending. **Not interested** sends the visit to another field agent and tells the manager.
- [ ] **Check out** at the end of the day.

Visits are shared by kW: the field agent with the least work gets the next visit.

---

## 4. Staff / custom roles (no leads) — working with Instructions

If your role does not get leads, your work comes as **Instructions**.

1. Your phone shows **"New instruction from <name>: <task>"**.
2. Open **Instructions → For me**.
3. Update the status as you work, with a short **Note** if useful:

| Now | You can change it to |
|---|---|
| To do | In progress · On hold |
| In progress | **Send for review** (when done) · On hold |
| In review | In progress (if you need to fix something) |
| On hold | In progress |

4. When you finish, press **Send for review**. The person who gave the task checks it and marks it **Completed**. You cannot mark Completed yourself.
5. Items past their due date show **(late)** in red — do them first.

You can also give an instruction to a colleague: **+ New instruction**.

**Daily checklist — staff**
- [ ] Open **Instructions → For me** in the morning.
- [ ] Start the urgent / late ones first; set them **In progress**.
- [ ] Send finished work **for review** with a note.

## 5. Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I get no leads | Check in. Not on break? Ask your manager to check **Gets leads automatically** and the lead order. |
| Customer number shows as ●●●● | Accept the lead first. |
| I checked out by mistake | Tap **Check in** again. |
| My manager pinged me | The message shows as a notification; it is also on the lead's timeline. |
| WhatsApp send from CRM fails | The customer has not messaged in 24 hours — use the WhatsApp button to chat from your phone. |
